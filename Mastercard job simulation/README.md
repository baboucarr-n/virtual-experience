# Mastercard - Cybersecurity Job Simulation

Virtual Experience Program - Forage
Completed: 22 November 2026

## What I did

Designed a phishing email simulation, analysed phishing results to find the most vulnerable teams, and learned how to spot and stop phishing attacks.

## Key finding

HR was the most vulnerable team - 100% opened, 85% clicked, 75% gave info.
IT was the least vulnerable - 80% opened, 2% clicked, 0% gave info.

Open rate alone does not mean vulnerable. Success rate is what matters.
